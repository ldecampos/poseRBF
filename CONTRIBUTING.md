# Open-source, not open-contribution

This is a one-person project, written in the time a full-time job leaves over.
It is open source under the MIT license, and it is **not open to contributions**.

That is a deliberate choice, not an oversight. Reviewing a change, agreeing on
its shape and standing behind it afterwards costs more than the time this
project has.

## What is welcome

**A bug report.** What you did, what happened, what you expected, and which
Maya and Bifrost you were in. A scene that reproduces it is worth more than a
paragraph describing one.

**An idea.** What you were trying to do and where the tool got in the way. The
problem is more useful than a proposed solution: the same problem often has a
better answer than the one that first comes to mind.

Both go here:

[![issue tracker](https://img.shields.io/badge/bugs_and_ideas-open_an_issue-blue.svg?logo=github)](https://github.com/ldecampos/poseRBF/issues/new)

## What that promises

**Nothing.** Reports and ideas are read when there is time. Many will get no
reply, and a reply is not a commitment to act. An issue may sit open
indefinitely, or be closed without being fixed.

## If you want a change made

Fork it. The MIT license exists for exactly this: copy it, change it, ship it,
commercially or otherwise, without asking anyone.

Nothing has to come back here. If you build something worth seeing, an issue
saying so is welcome — as a pointer, not as a submission.
